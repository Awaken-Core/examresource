# Homepage (`/`) Design Rules

## Structure
1. **Masthead / Hero**: Clean introduction with search bar and key value proposition.
2. **Notice Desk & Attempt Sheets**: Two-column entry point into editorial articles and live tests.
3. **Practice Trend & Exam Streams**: Interactive forecast trend widget and categorized stream pathways.
4. **Official Commission Portals**: 6-column quick links to UPSC, SSC, RRB, IBPS, and State PSCs.

## Constraints
- Hero search bar must support keyboard shortcuts (`/` and `Ctrl+K`).
- Maintain 95+ PageSpeed score: All non-critical widgets must be non-blocking.
